# Code of Conduct

AOR follows the Contributor Covenant spirit for respectful, productive open source collaboration.

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, tradeoffs, and project goals;
- avoid harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks;
- respect privacy and never disclose secrets, credentials, private repository details, or private communications without permission.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project spaces, and any other AOR community venue controlled by the maintainers.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; temporarily
restrict participation; or permanently ban contributors when behavior harms
the project or community. Enforcement decisions should consider context,
severity, recurrence, and the safety of affected community members.

## Reporting and Privacy

For conduct on GitHub, use GitHub's private
[Report Abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For a
project-specific concern, open a minimal issue requesting confidential
maintainer contact, without including names, screenshots, private messages, or
other sensitive details. A dedicated project conduct address is not yet
published; the repository's vulnerability-reporting form is reserved for
security vulnerabilities.

Maintainers will limit report details to people needed for investigation and
will avoid assigning a reported maintainer to decide their own case. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant is itself a violation. Security issues must
follow `SECURITY.md` and must not be submitted as conduct reports.
